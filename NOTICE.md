## Project history

The original SCD2 physical platform was produced by a four-person engineering team. Jonathan C. Hernandez served as team lead. The post-handoff dual-core firmware integration, timestamped acquisition, host application, automated test workflows, characterization tooling, and public technical reports were developed and organized under Herder Elektronische Systemen.
