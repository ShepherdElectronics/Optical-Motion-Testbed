# Power-up, shutdown, and acceptance

1. Isolate power; verify E-stop access and clear travel.
2. Record microstep, current, and standby-current switch positions.
3. Verify encoder-pair polarity and seven-pin connector order.
4. Apply logic power and confirm stable encoder data.
5. Apply motor-driver power and check for unexpected motion.
6. Run a low-speed positive/negative direction check.
7. Verify at least +/-3 in travel without collision or binding.
8. Test the E-stop and document results.
9. Command zero speed before shutdown; remove motor power before logic power or service.

The 40 lb load result is a static demonstration, not a certified dynamic payload rating.
